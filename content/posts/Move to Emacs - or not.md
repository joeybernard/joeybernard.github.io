date: 2026-10-13

I had been experimenting with using Emacs as my main information tool. Org-mode is amazingly useful. And the complete idea of having a computational environment that can be changed on the fly is also very cool. But, part of my problem is that I am constantly moving between Windows and Linux PCs,  and Android tablets. With Obsidian plus sync, I can always get access to all of my notes and information. So, I guess I'll stick with Obsidian. At least for now.
